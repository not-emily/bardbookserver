Reaching Bard from outside

Bard is listening on {{.BindAddr}} with tls.mode: {{.TLSMode}}.

Remote access is a deployment choice rather than a setting here: a Cloudflare Tunnel or a reverse proxy terminates TLS in front of Bard, and only a direct port-forward wants Bard's own certificates. Changing it means editing bard.yml and restarting, so this page reports the setting rather than rewriting a file you may have commented.

If anything sits in front of Bard, set trusted_proxies. Too permissive lets a caller forge its address past login rate limiting; too strict collapses every client into one bucket.
